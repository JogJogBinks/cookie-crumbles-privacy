Cookie Crumbles privacy

Wood Technologies LLC · Version 1.1 · Updated November 22, 2026

Offline play and local progress

Solo progress, chapter stars, coins, supplies, outfits, shoes, personal records, settings, Story choices, Tin badges and stickers are saved on your device. Cookie Cup also keeps local course records, rewards, unlocks, control and quick-chat preferences, and ladder progress associated with a device-specific key for your Game Center account. Reminder choices and the local notification schedule stay on your device. Offline play needs no account. The game includes no advertising, analytics SDK or tracking across apps. Deleting the app or clearing its saved data removes local progress.

Optional purchases

Cookie Crumbles is free to play, with optional in-app purchases. Every world and chapter is included. Optional App Store purchases add coin packs, a starter bundle, the Coin doubler or the Legends wardrobe. There are no subscriptions. There are no loot boxes. Apple processes every purchase. Wood Technologies does not receive payment-card details or your Apple Account password. The app receives verified product and transaction information to deliver purchases and restore permanent entitlements. Consumable coins and supplies stay in the current device save.

Optional Game Center rankings on iPhone and iPad

A fresh install starts in offline mode. When you choose rankings or online races, the game checks your Game Center account. If you previously enabled online racing, the app can reconnect when it opens. A sign-in sheet is shown only after you choose to connect. Opening the existing 60-second leaderboard does not publish a score: choose to connect to those rankings, then explicitly start a ranked run that posts its result. Practice never posts. Game Center stores the participating player's game-specific identifier, display alias and submitted scores. The game displays the global top 30 plus your own best score and rank, even when you are outside the top 30. Other participating players can see the displayed alias and score. Wood Technologies can view leaderboard aliases and scores in App Store Connect to manage the rankings. The app does not request your real name, email, contacts or location for this feature.

A failed or pending submission is stored locally for the same Game Center player. It is never reassigned to a different Apple player after an account change. Your Game Center profile and its controls are managed through Apple. Deleting this app does not delete a score already held by Game Center. Contact support with questions about a score or your data. We do not sell gameplay or profile data.

Optional Cookie Cup online races

Offline Cup Practice needs no account and sends no race or chat data to other players. If you turn on online play, Apple Game Center connects Quick race, Ranked and friend Party matches. Quick races and supported parties can connect up to four real players. Ranked races use two real players. The game labels computer racers separately. Game Center supplies player aliases and the identity needed to connect the match. Players exchange temporary race information, such as readiness, movement, item actions and results, plus selected preset quick-chat line identifiers. Other players can see the displayed alias and selected lines. Eligible racers can also exchange short typed messages and voice clips using Match chat. Messages are visible only to participants in the current match.

Cup Ranked races between people update your local ladder and submit the eligible rating to a separate Game Center leaderboard. Game Center holds the posted alias, rating and rank. Local account keys and pending results prevent a result from being posted for a different player after an account change. Multiplayer restrictions disable online races; communication-restricted and underage accounts cannot send or see Cup quick chat. You can turn online play off and change name/chat display choices in Controls and comfort. The game does not request your real name, email, contacts or location for online play.

Match chat, voice and player controls

In a real Game Center race with other people, eligible players can use the match-side feed to type short English messages, even in Quick Play. The last 24 messages stay in memory for this match. Leaving clears the feed. Turn text off to clear it sooner. Each sender and receiver checks messages on their own device before sending or showing them. Natural racing conversation uses Apple’s on-device language model when available to the participants. With an unavailable model, use one of the listed race lines. The preset quick-chat wheel also works without microphone access.

Voice is off until you choose Enable match voice and grant microphone and speech-recognition permission. Tapping Speak captures up to 2.4 seconds in memory. English speech recognition and any language-model check run on the phone. Only screened clips go to other voice-enabled racers over Game Center. Their phones independently check the words before playback. Voice stops when you turn it off, leave the match or put the app in the background. The game saves no audio or transcript. Wood Technologies does not receive race chat or voice clips. Underage and communication-restricted Game Center accounts cannot use match chat.

Mute another racer for this match or block their chat on this device. A block uses a locally scoped key on this device. Apple supplies a temporary identifier for some opponents, so those blocks last until the app restarts. Open the Game Center profile for Apple account controls. Reporting to Wood Technologies opens an editable email containing the displayed nickname and time. Only your mail app sends the report. Audio and match history are never attached automatically. If you send a report, support receives your email address, the information in your report and any attachments you personally add, and uses them to investigate and respond.

FaceTime and SharePlay

On supported devices, friends already in a FaceTime call can choose Start SharePlay party when it is available in Cup. Apple supplies the activity participants needed to form the game match. Conversation stays in FaceTime, under Apple's controls. Cookie Crumbles does not receive or save the FaceTime call audio. Match voice has its own controls and permissions. Ending the game's SharePlay offering does not end an established race or the FaceTime call.

Optional local reminders

After you claim a daily treat, Remind me asks for notification permission; Not now does not. You can also manage Treat reminder in the game's settings. Choose an hour from 08:00 to 20:00, with 18:00 as the default. At most one reminder a day can tell you that a treat, the first Cup race bonus, or both are waiting. Notifications are scheduled locally on the device. This feature uses no reminder server or push token and sends no reminder or gameplay information to us. Turning the setting off clears the game's pending and delivered reminders. You can also manage notification permission in iOS Settings.

Optional iPhone Duo hinge controls

Cookie Tin can use hinge input on supported devices after you choose Use Duo hinge and set a comfortable starting position. Angles and that starting position are temporary; they are not saved as a history or uploaded. Observation stops on exit, completion or backgrounding, and returning requires an explicit reconnect. Tin and Cup can check whether a hinge has been seen to offer supported controls and layouts without retaining angles. A completed Tin round saves its cosmetic badge, not the readings. Touch controls remain available.

Local saves and deletion

Story runs locally as authored content; choices are not sent to an AI service. Deleting the app removes its local Story, Tin, Cup and reminder records along with other local progress. The app does not sync these saves between devices. Deleting local data does not delete Game Center scores or ratings already held by Apple or a support message you chose to send.

Browser edition

The browser edition's optional ranking service is separate from Game Center and may be unavailable. If you choose to join that service, a generated browser identifier and alias are used, and its on-screen public-data notice describes the score and replay handling. Browser practice sends no score. The browser service is not used by the iOS app.

Optional level suggestions

Suggest a level lets you write an optional idea and open it as a draft in your Mail app. Cookie Crumbles does not send the draft, save the form to your game progress or attach gameplay data. You choose the sending account and decide whether to send in Mail. If you send it, support receives your email address and the message you include. Suggestions are not posted publicly. You can use the support page instead, or simply close the form and keep playing.
