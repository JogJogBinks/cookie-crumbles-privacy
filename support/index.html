Version 1.1 · Wood Technologies LLC

How do I play?

Choose Chapter journey for the campaign or Cookie Cup → Practice for an offline race against computer bakers. Single player and Practice work offline. Your coins and progress save on the device.

Swipe left or right
Steer. In the campaign, swipe toward the arrow when a corner approaches.
Swipe up
Jump.
Swipe down
Slide.
Tap the track
Use a held Cup item. In solo play, tap to drop ice when you have some.

In a Cup race, follow just behind a baker in the same lane for a small speed boost. Use Pause for a break.

How do I race a friend?

Open Cookie Cup → Race online, enable online races and connect to Game Center. Choose Party for an invited race. Both players need a compatible build. In online races, preset chat follows the account’s Game Center communication restrictions.

What if the game closes?

For a TestFlight copy, open TestFlight, find Cookie Crumbles and tap Update if offered. If it closes again, use Send Beta Feedback and include your build number, iPhone model, iOS version and the last thing you saw. An App Store copy can send those details to our support email. Keeping the app installed preserves its local save.
